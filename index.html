HeadCheck VR

A 2½-minute virtual delivery ride. It measures whether a rider checks over their shoulder before turning or changing lane, and how fast they spot hazards.

Checking VR support…

Say this to the rider

  1. "Ride as you normally would on a real road."
  2. "Tap the headset button as soon as you spot a hazard."
  3. Do not mention shoulder checks – the test measures what they do naturally.

Operator steps

  1. Seat the rider on a swivel chair so they can turn freely.
  2. Tap Start in VR headset, put the phone in the Procus, hand it over.
  3. The rider looks straight ahead and taps (or waits 8 s) to begin.
  4. When the ride ends, take the phone out and save the result.
