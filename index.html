Checking VR support…
How the rider controls the scooter
  • Tilt the head left or right to lean and steer, like on a real bike.
  • Hold the headset button to brake. Let go to ride on.
  • Tap the button whenever the small red light appears at the edge of view.
  • Remember the house number announced at the start.

Without a headset: drag to look, A / D or ← / → to lean, Space to brake and tap, 1 2 3 to answer. On a phone, tilt the phone to steer and touch the screen to brake.
